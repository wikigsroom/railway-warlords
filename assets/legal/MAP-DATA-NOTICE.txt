Map geography: Natural Earth public-domain land and rivers; SRTM v3 90m elevation queried via OpenTopoData; ENP-China Republican period provincial vectors by Christian Henriot and Pierre-Henri Dubois, released CC0.
Source URLs, sampled-data hashes, date corrections and limits are recorded in content/geography_sources.json.
Historical names and factual notes are paraphrased from the listed primary and official sources; no source photos or protected illustrations are incorporated.
City/railway markers and five-point relief classification are declared strategic abstractions, not survey-grade 1920 cadastral, coastline or troop-disposition reconstruction.
Additional historical settlement positions and attribution are documented in GEONAMES-NOTICE.txt and presentation/data/settlements_v023.json.
Current game control colors follow actual in-game city ownership over sourced dated province polygons and surveyed land. Contested provinces are subdivided by city influence. This is a gameplay overlay, not a surveyed historical military or county boundary reconstruction; Jingzhao county membership is documented without inventing an unavailable border polygon.
