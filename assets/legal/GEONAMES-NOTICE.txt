Changes: selected same-name populated-place coordinates, projected into the game map; historical names and administrative ranks are separately sourced from contemporary government bulletins and official local histories. No modern administrative polygons or ranks are substituted for the 1920-1924 setting.
The original CN.txt dump and readme are retained in the source/provenance archive under design/geodata/settlements/.
Three village locations without a matched point are explicitly coarse coordinates based on official locality descriptions, with 1500m positioning precision; they are not exact old-government-office survey points.
